Immediate responses, exceptional driveability, and greater fuel efficiency. The 48 V mild hybrid system enhances the V8 engine with improved torque and sprint performance.

Extreme performance demands extreme stopping power. Equipped with Defender OCTA-specific brake pads, calipers and brake discs, the new Integrated Power Brake system reduces brake fade.

Defender OCTA features an active exhaust system that allows for optimum thermal management in OCTA mode and gives the vehicle a distinctive roar.
